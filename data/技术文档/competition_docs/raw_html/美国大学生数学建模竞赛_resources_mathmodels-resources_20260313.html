Teachers and students use the materials found on this site to enrich their classes and prepare for mathematical modeling competitions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to be a member to view modeling problems?

No, Mathmodels problems are free for classroom use.

When are new problems added?

Typically, new problems are added after the current contest is over.

When are student solutions available for download?

Typically, student solutions are available after the contest results are announced.

When will problem commentaries be available for downlaod?

Typically, commentaries are available 2-3 months after the contest results are announced.

I forgot my member username and/or password. How can I access my account?

If you have a COMAP membership, go to the login page and click the appropriate link for Forgot your username or Forgot your password.

Enter the email address used to register your account, and you'll be sent an email with instructions. If there's a problem with the email you used to register, contact us for more help.

Can you help me with PDF files?

Portable Document Format (PDF) is a versatile file format created by Adobe that gives people an easy, reliable way to present and exchange documents - regardless of the software, hardware, or operating systems. If your system is not already able to open, view, and print PDF files, you can download and install Adobe's Acrobat Reader.

What materials does my Mathmodels.org account give me access too?

As a Mathmodels.org member, you will receive an electronic subscription to all the Mathmodels problems, student papers and commentaries. For a complete list click here.

Can I duplicate COMAP materials for my classroom use?

A Membership comes with a license to duplicate (in print form only) any of the materials for use within classes the COMAP member teaches. This grants users a license to duplicate materials from this site for non-profit or educational purposes only. These materials may not be sold commercially for profit, bartered, or given to anyone other than students attending courses at the purchasing institution or faculty teaching these courses. These materials may not be modified or used in any other manner without the expressed written permission of COMAP. The materials must be attributed/cited to COMAP, Inc.

Will COMAP automatically charge for annual renewal?

No, we do not automatically charge for membership renewal. Members will receive an email with a reminder prior to their membership expiring.

Do you offer institutional Mathmodels.org accounts?

Yes, in addition to schools and academic institutions, COMAP offers a wide range of institutional membership options to a variety of organizations, libraries, and industry/business entities.
